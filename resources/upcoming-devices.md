### 3D-printing notes

The combined STL contains a rear enclosure, fabric-covered front mask, and a
fabric-wrapping helper. Only the rear enclosure remains visible, so the mask and
helper can use any available filament colour. The floating middle component can
require unnecessary supports unless the objects are separated and repositioned.
A useful upstream improvement would be publishing the three components as
separate STL files.
